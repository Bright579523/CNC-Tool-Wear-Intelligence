About this model · evidence

What it does. A standardized linear Ridge regression model (α = 1.0) estimates face-milling flank wear (VB, mm) from 3 machining-context inputs and 5 sensor features. It was benchmarked on the NASA milling dataset (145 valid observations, 16 tools, 8 cutting conditions).

Leave-one-tool-out (LOGO)MAE 0.1044 mmR² 0.6989 across 16 unseen tools
Leave-one-condition-out (LOCO)MAE 0.1221 mmR² 0.6360 across 8 unseen cutting regimes
Cross-checkGBDT + TreeSHAPdirectional consistency check

Limitations. Laboratory benchmark with face-milling inserts under dry cutting. Inputs outside observed ranges represent extrapolation. Benchmark metrics represent grouped cross-validation error, not full-data training residual error. Not a calibrated confidence interval.
